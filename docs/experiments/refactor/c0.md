the global setting, without mutating authored settings.

or skipped supervision; `executed_positions` counts globally forwarded padded
positions, including AMP-skipped forwards. Successful optimizer updates drive
`global_step`, schedules and cadences. Logging resets on every rank; output
and transport remain main-rank work. The global weighted eligible-token loss
denominator is unchanged, including partial windows and empty modalities/ranks.
